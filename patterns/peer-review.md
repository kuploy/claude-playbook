# Peer review

Use: normal work on a repo that already runs.

## Roles
- writer: builds, and owns the PR thread.
- reviewer: two peers, neither of them the writer.

## Writers and branches
- Writers: 1.
- Open branches per writer: 1, named `feat/<role>/<n>-slug`, where `<role>` is the session's role name, such as `console-writer`.

## Reviews
- Reviews before merge: 2, one from each reviewer.

## Reports to Ed
- The writer.

## Spawn cap and archiving
- Spawn cap: 2
- The writer may spawn up to 2 short-lived reviewers, after asking Ed. Reviewers don't spawn.
- The writer archives each reviewer once its review is posted.
